# Rooms, Doors and Lighting

The room tools live in Foundry's **Walls** group.

## Drawing a room

Choose **Room**.

- **Click** each corner. Corners snap to the grid.
- **Right-click** to finish the room. A room needs at least three corners.

A room gets native walls around its edge and a light at its centre. Rooms that touch share their walls. A new room takes the materials last chosen in the *Room materials* panel.

## Room materials

Choose **Room materials** and click a room to open the panel.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Floor** | The floor: any ground, or a floor material from the active texture set. |
| **Walls** | *Not drawn*, or a wall material from the texture set, drawn along the walls. |
| **Wall kind** | Which of Foundry's wall kinds the room's walls are: Solid, Terrain, Invisible, Ethereal or Window. |
| **Ceiling (when a level is above)** | When the scene has a level above this room's, the room gets a ceiling region named *Ceiling of* that level. Untick for an open courtyard or a shaft. |

Your choice also becomes the default for the next room you draw.

## Doors

Choose **Doors**.

- **Click** a room's wall to put a door there.
- **Click** an existing door to open the *Door* panel.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Door type** | *Door*, *Secret door*, or *Opening (no door, no wall)*. |
| **Door state** | *Closed*, *Open* or *Locked*. |
| **Door sound** | *Foundry default*, or one of Foundry's door sounds. |
| **Door animation** | *Foundry default*, or Ascend, Descend, Slide, Swing or Swivel. |
| **Remove door** | Turns the door back into wall. |

Door stamps (see [Stamps](?path=/docs/guide-stamps--docs)) dropped close to a room's wall snap onto it and cut the wall themselves.

## Light switches

A light-switch stamp works as a switch for lamps, rooms and lights.

1. Choose **Link light switches** in Foundry's **Lighting** group.
2. Click a switch to pick it.
3. Click each lamp stamp, room or Foundry light it should control. Clicking one again unlinks it. Clicking another switch picks that switch instead.

Link lines show only while the tool is active. In play, a switch is worked like a door, with its own light icon. When it is on, linked lamps change to their lit look, rooms turn their centre light on, and linked Foundry lights appear.

## Daylight

Rooms built by the Map builder, or from a scene spec, can have windows that let in daylight. Each one gets a warm light just outside it, shining into the room, which is on only while the scene is light enough (darkness 0.6 or less).

## Drop shadows

With the **Drop shadows** setting on, standing stamps cast a soft shadow away from the scene's sun. The default sun is high in the north-west. A game system or macro can move it through the module's API (see [Map Builder](?path=/docs/guide-map-builder--docs)). No shadows fall while the sun is down. Shadows are drawn on each player's screen and are not saved in the scene.
