# Regions, Pins, Labels and Zones

## Area effects

Choose **Area effects** in Foundry's **Regions** group, and click painted ground, a room or a zone.

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Movement cost (×)** | How many times the normal movement cost the area costs, 0 to 5. |
| **Region** | The area's Foundry region settings: visibility, highlighting, measurements, whether players observe it, *Hidden: GM only, behaviours off*, and whether walls of a chosen kind shape it. |
| **Region behaviours** | Add Foundry region behaviours with **New behaviour** and **Add behaviour**: Adjust Darkness Level, Suppress Weather, Display Scrolling Text, Pause Game, Execute Macro, Execute Script, Apply Active Effect and Toggle Behavior. **Remove** takes one away. |
| **Spawn** | Tokens to put into the area. List actors one per line, as a UUID or as a count then a UUID. Choose whether they **Arrive** *At the centre* or *Anywhere inside*, whether to **Snap to the grid** and **Avoid occupied spaces**, then click **Spawn now**. |

## Map pins

Choose **Map pin** in Foundry's **Notes** group.

- **Click** empty canvas to place a pin; its panel opens.
- **Click** a pin to change it.

The panel sets the pin's text, the journal entry and page it opens, its icon and icon size, and Foundry's *global* option (shown whether or not a token can see it). **Players read it on hover** turns it into a sign: no icon is drawn, and players read its text by hovering.

A pin can be **Hidden from players**: tick or untick this on Foundry's own Note sheet. Players do not see a hidden pin until you untick it.

## Map labels

Choose **Map label** in Foundry's **Drawings** group.

- **Click** to place text on the map, or click a label to change it.

The panel sets the text, font size (8 to 256), colour, font, **Rotation (°)**, and **Hidden from players**.

## Zones

A zone is a region in one of Foundry's shapes, for a blast, an aura or an area hazard.

Choose **Zone** in Foundry's **Regions** group.

- **Click** to place a zone, a circle 150 pixels across, and open its panel.
- **Click** a zone to reshape it.

The panel sets its name, shape (circle, ellipse, ring, cone, line, rectangle, grid spaces on square grids, or emanation), size, rotation, and whether it follows the grid. **Attached token** makes the zone follow a token on the same level as it moves.

### Hazard presets

Save a zone you have set up as a preset: give it a **Preset name** and click **Save this zone as a preset**. To reuse it, pick it under **Preset** and click **Apply to this zone**. **Forget preset** deletes it. Presets are saved in the world.

Use **Area effects** on a zone to give it a movement cost, behaviours or spawns.
