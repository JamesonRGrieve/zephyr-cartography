# Zephyr's Cartography

Zephyr's Cartography is a scene-authoring module for [Foundry Virtual Tabletop](https://foundryvtt.com/). You paint terrain, draw roads and rivers, build rooms and place stamps directly on the canvas. Everything it makes is an ordinary Foundry document: walls, doors, lights, tiles, regions, notes, drawings and levels. Vision, movement and lighting therefore work as they do on any hand-built scene, and the scene keeps working if the module is later disabled.

It requires **Foundry VTT version 14** and works with any game system.

## What it does

- **Terrain:** paint thirteen kinds of ground with a round brush, blend them together, and bake the result into a tile or a level's background.
- **Roads and rivers:** smooth paths of any width. Rivers can carry water, lava, poison or acid over a bed.
- **Rooms:** grid-snapped rooms with floor and wall materials, native walls, a centre light, doors and secret doors.
- **Stamps:** art from asset packs, placed as tiles that bring their own walls, lights, doors, stairs, containers, hazards and sounds.
- **Levels:** multi-floor scenes on Foundry's native Levels, joined by stairs, ladders, lifts and hatches.
- **Interiors:** link a building on an outdoor map to its own interior scene, with an entrance and an exit.
- **Regions, pins, labels and zones:** movement costs, region behaviours, token spawns, map notes, text labels and shaped hazard zones.
- **Map builder:** compose a whole map from a short description, generate a floor plan from a seed, or build any map written as a scene spec.

## No art is included

The module bundles no images or sounds. Stamps and terrain textures come from separate asset-pack modules. Without a pack, terrain, floors and roads draw as procedural patterns in each ground's colour, and the stamp browser is empty. See [Asset Packs](?path=/docs/guide-asset-packs--docs).
